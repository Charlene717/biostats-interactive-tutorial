Step 8

線性回歸

線性回歸尋求兩個變數之間的最佳直線關係，計算斜率與截距並解釋模型決定係數。

Linear regression finds the best‑fitting linear relationship between two variables, computing the slope and intercept and interpreting the coefficient of determination.

概念

最小平方法

簡單線性回歸假定自變項 x 與因變項 y 之間呈線性關係 y=β₀+β₁x+ε。透過最小平方法選取斜率 β₁ 和截距 β₀，使預測值與觀察值的平方差總和最小。決定係數 R² 表示模型解釋總變異的比例。

The simple linear regression model assumes a linear relationship between the predictor x and response y: y=β₀+β₁x+ε. The least‑squares method chooses the slope β₁ and intercept β₀ to minimise the sum of squared deviations between predicted and observed values. The coefficient of determination R² indicates the proportion of total variation explained by the model.
