Step 11

檢定力與樣本數

研究設計必須考慮檢定力：足夠的樣本數可降低犯第二型錯誤的機率。

Study design must consider statistical power: having enough participants reduces the probability of making a type II error.

概念

檢定力

檢定力 (power) 為 1−β，表示在真實存在效應時拒絕零假設的機率。檢定力太低會增加第二型錯誤（漏檢）的風險。規劃研究時需決定可接受的 α 與 β，以及預期效果大小與變異，據此計算所需樣本數。

Statistical power is defined as 1−β, the probability of rejecting the null hypothesis when a true effect exists. Low power increases the risk of a type II error (failing to detect a real effect). When planning a study one must set acceptable α and β and specify the expected effect size and variability to compute the required sample size.

互動

計算樣本數

假設欲比較兩組平均，輸入預期的效果大小 Δ、標準差 σ、顯著水準 α 以及期望檢定力 (1−β)。計算公式 n = 2·((z1−α/2 + z1−β)·σ/Δ)2。結果為每組所需樣本數。

For comparing two means, enter the expected effect size Δ, the standard deviation σ, the significance level α and the desired power (1−β). The required sample size per group is n = 2·((z1−α/2 + z1−β)·σ/Δ)2.
