Step 11

檢定力與樣本數

研究設計必須考慮檢定力：足夠的樣本數可降低犯第二型錯誤的機率【358404025352162†L312-L347】。

Study design must consider power: sufficient sample size reduces the probability of making a type II error【358404025352162†L312-L347】.

概念

檢定力

檢定力 (power) 為 1−β，表示在真實存在效應時拒絕零假設的機率。如果檢定力太低，可能導致第二型錯誤 (false negative)【358404025352162†L312-L347】。規劃研究時需決定可接受的 α 與 β、效果大小與變異，計算所需樣本數。

Power is 1−β, the probability of rejecting the null hypothesis when a true effect exists. Low power increases the risk of a type II error【358404025352162†L312-L347】. When planning a study, one must specify acceptable α and β, the expected effect size and variability to determine the required sample size.

互動

計算樣本數

假設欲比較兩組平均，輸入預期的效果大小 Δ、標準差 σ、顯著水準 α 以及期望檢定力 (1−β)。計算公式 \(n=2((z_{1-α/2}+z_{1-β})\,σ/Δ)^2\)。結果為每組所需樣本數。

For comparing two means, enter the expected effect size Δ, the standard deviation σ, the significance level α and the desired power (1−β). The required sample size per group is \(n=2((z_{1-α/2}+z_{1-β})\,σ/Δ)^2\).
