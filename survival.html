Step 10

存活分析

專門處理隨機事件發生時間資料的統計方法，需考慮截尾現象與不同的追蹤時間。

A field dedicated to analysing time‑to‑event data, taking account of censoring and varying follow‑up times.

概念

存活函數與截尾

存活分析研究事件發生時間的分布。資料常受到截尾：若在研究期結束前或失訪時尚未發生事件，只能知道存活時間至少超過某值。存活函數 S(t)=P(T>t) 描述個體存活超過時間 t 的機率，通常以 Kaplan–Meier 法加以估計。

Survival analysis examines how long it takes for an event to occur. Data are often censored: if the event has not happened before the end of follow‑up or if the subject is lost, we only know that the survival time is at least beyond a given value. The survival function S(t)=P(T>t) gives the probability of surviving beyond time t and is commonly estimated with the Kaplan–Meier method.
