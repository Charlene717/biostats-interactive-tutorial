存活分析

專門處理隨機事件發生時間資料，需考慮截尾現象與不同追蹤時間【989822755655977†L32-L39】。

A field dedicated to analysing time‑to‑event data while accounting for censoring and variable follow‑up【989822755655977†L32-L39】.

概念

存活函數與截尾

存活分析研究事件發生的時間分布。資料常受到截尾：若在研究期結束前或失訪時尚未發生事件，僅知道存活時間超過某值【989822755655977†L73-L80】。存活函數 S(t)=P(T>t) 描述個體存活超過時間 t 的機率，常以 Kaplan–Meier 法估計【989822755655977†L99-L120】。

Survival analysis studies the distribution of times until an event occurs. Data are often censored: if the event has not occurred by the end of follow‑up or if the subject is lost, we only know that the survival time exceeds a certain value【989822755655977†L73-L80】. The survival function S(t)=P(T>t) gives the probability that an individual survives beyond time t; it is often estimated using the Kaplan–Meier method【989822755655977†L99-L120】.
