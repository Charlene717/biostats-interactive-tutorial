存活分析

專門處理隨機事件發生時間資料的統計方法，需考慮截尾現象與不同的追蹤時間。

A field dedicated to analysing time‑to‑event data, taking account of censoring and varying follow‑up times.

概念

存活函數與截尾

存活分析研究事件發生時間的分布。資料常受到截尾：若在研究期結束前或失訪時尚未發生事件，只能知道存活時間至少超過某值。存活函數 S(t)=P(T>t) 描述個體存活超過時間 t 的機率，通常以 Kaplan–Meier 法加以估計。

Survival analysis examines how long it takes for an event to occur. Data are often censored: if the event has not happened before the end of follow‑up or if the subject is lost, we only know that the survival time is at least beyond a given value. The survival function S(t)=P(T>t) gives the probability of surviving beyond time t and is commonly estimated with the Kaplan–Meier method.

互動

Kaplan–Meier 曲線

輸入多個存活時間與事件指示 (1=發生事件, 0=截尾)，以逗號分隔。計算並繪製存活曲線。

Enter several survival times and event indicators (1=event occurred, 0=censored) separated by commas. The survival curve will be computed and plotted.

優缺點與限制

優勢與局限

存活分析能解析事件發生時間，尤其適用於具截尾資料的研究。以下列出此方法的主要優點與限制：

Survival analysis models time to an event, particularly when data include censoring. The following are key advantages and limitations:

優點

  • 以事件發生的時間為核心，提供何時發生事件的洞見，有助於風險評估與策略規劃。
  • 能妥善處理截尾資料，不僅利用已觀察到的事件，也保留未發生事件的個體資訊。
  • 可選擇不同模型，如 Kaplan–Meier、Cox 比例風險、參數模型等，靈活度高。
  • 可結合多種指標或共變數進行風險預測，常用於金融、醫學等領域。
  • Focuses on the timing of events, offering insights into when events are likely to occur and aiding risk assessment and planning.
  • Handles censored data effectively, incorporating information from individuals whose event has not yet occurred.
  • Allows flexible model choice—Kaplan–Meier, Cox proportional hazards, or parametric models—depending on study needs.
  • Enables risk prediction using multiple covariates and is widely applied in finance, medicine and engineering.

缺點與限制

  • 需要蒐集高品質的時間事件資料，若資料不完整或失訪嚴重可能影響結果。
  • 許多模型建立在假設之上，例如截尾與存活時間獨立、風險比不隨時間改變等，若不符合會造成偏差。
  • 模型建構與解釋較為複雜，需專業知識，且不適用於非時間到事件的問題。
  • 在含大量共變數時容易過度擬合；Kaplan–Meier 方法為描述性，不可調整共變數。
  • Requires high‑quality time‑to‑event data; incomplete follow‑up or heavy loss to follow‑up may bias results.
  • Relies on assumptions such as non‑informative censoring and, for certain models, proportional hazards; violations can lead to biased estimates.
  • Models can be complex to implement and interpret and are not suitable for problems unrelated to time‑to‑event analysis.
  • May overfit when many covariates are included; the Kaplan–Meier estimator is descriptive and cannot adjust for covariates.
決策與範例

何時使用存活分析

存活分析適用於處理時間到事件資料，以下提供使用建議：

Survival analysis is used for time‑to‑event data. Below are some guidelines:

  • 研究結局應為事件發生所需的時間，例如疾病復發、機械故障等。
  • 資料中可能存在右截尾（截至追蹤結束仍未發生事件）或其他類型截尾，模型假設截尾與存活時間獨立。
  • 若只需描述存活曲線，可使用 Kaplan–Meier 法；若需加入共變數，則使用 Cox 比例風險模型或參數模型。
  • 確認樣本數足以提供穩定估計，並檢查共變數是否有共線性與時間依賴效應。
  • The outcome should be the time until an event occurs, such as relapse or failure.
  • Data may contain right censoring or other censoring types; assumptions of independence between censoring and survival must be reasonable.
  • Use the Kaplan–Meier estimator for descriptive curves; to include covariates, use a Cox proportional hazards or parametric model.
  • Ensure sufficient sample size for stable estimation and check for multicollinearity or time‑dependent effects among covariates.

實例

假設研究 100 名病人在治療後的復發時間，其中部分病人在研究期間內沒有復發（右截尾）。可使用 Kaplan–Meier 法估計存活曲線，計算中位存活時間，並用 log‑rank 檢定比較不同治療組；若考慮年齡、性別等共變數對復發的影響，可採用 Cox 比例風險模型。

Imagine following 100 patients after treatment to observe time to relapse, where some patients do not relapse during follow‑up (right censored). The Kaplan–Meier method can estimate the survival curve and median survival time, and the log‑rank test can compare treatment groups. If you want to adjust for age, sex or other covariates, use a Cox proportional hazards model.

R 與 Python 範例

存活分析程式碼

以下範例展示如何用 R 和 Python 估計 Kaplan–Meier 曲線並擬合 Cox 比例風險模型。

The examples below demonstrate how to estimate a Kaplan–Meier curve and fit a Cox proportional hazards model in R and Python.


# 載入 survival 套件
library(survival)
# 建立 Surv 物件並擬合 Kaplan–Meier 曲線
time <- c(5, 8, 12, 15, 20, 22)
event <- c(1, 0, 1, 1, 0, 1)
fitKM <- survfit(Surv(time, event) ~ 1)
plot(fitKM, xlab="Time", ylab="Survival Probability", main="Kaplan–Meier Curve")

# 建立含共變數的 Cox 模型
age <- c(45, 50, 55, 60, 65, 70)
modelCox <- coxph(Surv(time, event) ~ age)
summary(modelCox)
            

# simulate data and fit Kaplan–Meier and Cox models
import numpy as np
from lifelines import KaplanMeierFitter, CoxPHFitter

time = np.array([5, 8, 12, 15, 20, 22])
event = np.array([1, 0, 1, 1, 0, 1])
kmf = KaplanMeierFitter()
kmf.fit(time, event_observed=event)
kmf.plot_survival_function()

# fit a Cox proportional hazards model with a covariate (e.g. age)
age = np.array([45, 50, 55, 60, 65, 70])
import pandas as pd
df = pd.DataFrame({
    'time': time,
    'event': event,
    'age': age
})
cf = CoxPHFitter()
cf.fit(df, duration_col='time', event_col='event')
print(cf.params_)
