抽樣與中心極限定理

瞭解抽樣分布與中心極限定理，並透過模擬觀察樣本均值的分布趨近常態。

Understand sampling distributions and the Central Limit Theorem, and observe through simulation how sample means approximate a normal distribution.

概念

抽樣分布

當我們從母體抽取樣本並計算統計量（如樣本均值）時，這些統計量本身也具有分布，稱為抽樣分布。抽樣分布反映了在相同條件下無限次抽樣時統計量可能的變化情形。

When we draw samples from a population and compute statistics such as the sample mean, these statistics themselves have a distribution called the sampling distribution. The sampling distribution reflects how a statistic would vary if we repeatedly sample under the same conditions.

中心極限定理

中心極限定理指出：無論原始分布如何，只要樣本大小足夠大（一般至少 30），樣本均值的抽樣分布將近似常態分布，平均數約為母體平均 μ，標準差約為 σ/√n。

The Central Limit Theorem states that regardless of the shape of the original distribution, the sampling distribution of the sample mean will be approximately normal when the sample size is large enough (typically at least 30). The mean of this distribution is the population mean μ and its standard deviation is σ/√n.

優缺點與限制

抽樣分布與中心極限定理

優點：中心極限定理指出，當樣本大小足夠大時，樣本均值的分布會趨近常態，即使母體分布不是常態【42080992128730†L126-L141】。這讓我們可以使用常態分布的計算與表格來做推論分析，簡化了統計方法，並提供可預測的樣本均值行為【8861871106705†L71-L82】。缺點與限制：此定理需要樣本隨機且彼此獨立，且樣本數充足【42080992128730†L206-L221】。當樣本過小或母體極度偏斜或含有重尾時，樣本均值可能無法良好近似常態分布，且需要更大的樣本量【42080992128730†L126-L141】。此外，CLT 假設的是樣本均值，而非其他統計量；對中位數或範圍等量的抽樣分布可能有不同性質【42080992128730†L236-L250】。

Advantages: The central limit theorem states that, with a sufficiently large sample size, the distribution of sample means tends toward normal regardless of the population’s shape【42080992128730†L126-L141】. This allows us to use normal‑based tables and formulas for inference, simplifying statistical procedures and making the behaviour of sample means predictable【8861871106705†L71-L82】. Drawbacks & limitations: The CLT requires that observations are drawn randomly and independently and that the sample size is sufficiently large【42080992128730†L206-L221】. If the sample is too small or the population is extremely skewed or heavy‑tailed, the sampling distribution may not approximate normal well and larger samples are needed【42080992128730†L126-L141】. Also, the theorem applies specifically to sample means; other statistics like the median or range have different sampling distributions【42080992128730†L236-L250】.

決策引導與範例

在決定是否使用中心極限定理時，請確認數據是否隨機且獨立抽樣，並評估樣本大小是否足夠大。若母體分布接近對稱且無重尾，較小的樣本（例如 n=15）即可能讓樣本均值近似常態【42080992128730†L206-L221】；若母體偏態或重尾，則需更大的樣本，例如 n≥30 或 50 才足以應用 CLT【42080992128730†L126-L141】。例如，測量身高這類接近常態的變數，抽樣 15 人其平均就接近母體平均；而對於收入這種右偏分布，需抽取至少 30 或更多樣本其平均才會趨近常態。

When deciding whether to apply the CLT, make sure your data come from a random, independent sample and that the sample size is large enough. If the population distribution is approximately symmetric and not heavy‑tailed, even a modest sample (say n = 15) may produce a nearly normal sampling distribution【42080992128730†L206-L221】. If the population is skewed or has heavy tails, larger samples—30 or even 50 observations—are needed for the CLT to kick in【42080992128730†L126-L141】. For example, for a variable like height, which is roughly normal, the mean of 15 sampled individuals will be close to normal; for income, which is right‑skewed, you may need to sample at least 30 or more observations for the sample mean distribution to approximate normal.

程式範例

以下 R 與 Python 程式碼示範如何從指數分布（λ=1）隨機抽樣，計算樣本均值並繪製其分布，以觀察中心極限定理。點擊複製按鈕即可複製程式碼。

The R and Python snippets below demonstrate how to sample from an exponential distribution (λ=1), compute sample means and plot their distribution to illustrate the CLT. Click the copy icon to copy the code.

# 重複抽樣並繪製樣本均值分布
set.seed(123)
# 設定樣本大小 n 與抽樣次數 m
n <- 30; m <- 1000
# 使用 replicate 產生 m 組樣本均值
means <- replicate(m, mean(rexp(n, rate = 1)))
# 計算均值與標準差
print(mean(means)); print(sd(means))
# 繪製直方圖
hist(means, breaks = 30, main = 'Sampling distribution of means', xlab = 'sample mean')
# sample means from exponential distribution and plot histogram
import numpy as np
import matplotlib.pyplot as plt
# set sample size n and number of simulations m
n = 30; m = 1000
# generate m×n matrix of exponential samples
samples = np.random.exponential(scale=1, size=(m, n))
# compute sample means
means = samples.mean(axis=1)
# print mean and standard deviation
print(means.mean(), means.std())
# plot histogram of sample means
plt.hist(means, bins=30)
plt.xlabel('sample mean')
plt.title('Sampling distribution of means')
plt.show()
