假設檢定

了解零假設與對立假設、顯著水準與檢定統計，並透過範例演練檢定流程。

Understand null and alternative hypotheses, significance levels and test statistics, and walk through a testing procedure with an example.

概念

假設與檢定統計

假設檢定以零假設 (H₀) 與對立假設 (H₁) 為核心：H₀ 通常代表「無差異」或「效果為零」的主張，而 H₁ 則代表研究者欲證明的替代說法。決定顯著水準 α 後，計算樣本資料的檢定統計量並與臨界值比較，若結果落在拒絕區域則拒絕 H₀。

Hypothesis testing revolves around the null hypothesis (H₀), which usually represents no difference or no effect, and the alternative hypothesis (H₁), which represents what the researcher aims to demonstrate. After choosing a significance level α, we compute a test statistic from the sample data and compare it to a critical value. If the statistic falls into the rejection region, we reject H₀.
