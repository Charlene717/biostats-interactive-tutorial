Step 4

假設檢定

了解零假設與對立假設、顯著水準與檢定統計，並透過範例演練檢定流程。

Understand null and alternative hypotheses, significance levels and test statistics, and walk through a testing procedure with an example.

概念

假設與檢定統計

假設檢定以零假設 (H₀) 與對立假設 (H₁) 為核心：H₀ 通常代表「無差異」或「效果為零」的主張，而 H₁ 則代表研究者欲證明的替代說法。決定顯著水準 α 後，計算樣本資料的檢定統計量並與臨界值比較，若結果落在拒絕區域則拒絕 H₀。

Hypothesis testing revolves around the null hypothesis (H₀), which usually represents no difference or no effect, and the alternative hypothesis (H₁), which represents what the researcher aims to demonstrate. After choosing a significance level α, we compute a test statistic from the sample data and compare it to a critical value. If the statistic falls into the rejection region, we reject H₀.

互動

單樣本 t 檢定

輸入樣本均值、假設的母體均值、樣本標準差、樣本大小與顯著水準，計算 t 值並判斷是否拒絕 H₀。此處以標準常態分布近似 t 分布。

Enter the sample mean, hypothesised population mean, sample standard deviation, sample size and significance level to calculate the t‑statistic and decide whether to reject H₀. We use the normal approximation to the t distribution for simplicity.

優缺點與限制

假設檢定的優勢與侷限

假設檢定提供了 客觀且系統化 的流程，讓研究者能以樣本資料評估假設是否成立，避免單憑直覺做決策。它有助於資料驅動的決策與批判性思考，在商業、臨床試驗、製造及金融等領域廣泛應用，能提升營運效率並降低風險。

Hypothesis testing provides an objective and systematic framework for assessing whether a claim about a population is supported by sample data. By grounding decisions in statistical evidence rather than intuition, it improves data‑driven decision‑making and critical thinking, and is widely used across business, clinical, manufacturing and financial contexts to enhance operational efficiency and reduce risk.

限制與缺點包括：對 p 值的詮釋依賴停頓規則及多重比較設定，且不同檢定哲學混用可能產生概念混淆；聚焦於顯著性可能忽略估計與重覆驗證；出版偏差導致只報導顯著結果；若假設不合理或資料不符合獨立與隨機抽樣假設，檢定可能產生不可靠結論。

Limitations and drawbacks include: interpretation of p‑values depends on the stopping rule and handling of multiple comparisons; mixing Fisher’s and Neyman–Pearson philosophies can introduce conceptual confusion; focusing solely on statistical significance may distract from estimation and replication; publication bias may favour significant results; and if assumptions such as independence and random sampling are violated, the conclusions may be unreliable.

決策指引與範例

何時與如何進行假設檢定？

在實施假設檢定前，首先明確定義研究問題，擬定零假設 H₀ 與對立假設 H₁，並選擇合適的檢定類型（如 t 檢定、卡方檢定或 ANOVA）。 接著設定顯著水準 α—通常為 0.05，代表願意承擔 5% 的第一類錯誤風險。確保樣本來自隨機且獨立的觀測，並留意檢定的適用條件，例如 t 檢定須要資料近似常態分布。

Before conducting a hypothesis test, clearly define your research question and formulate the null hypothesis H₀ and alternative hypothesis H₁. Choose an appropriate test (e.g., t‑test, chi‑square test or ANOVA) and set the significance level α—commonly 0.05, representing a 5 % risk of a Type I error. Ensure that the sample is drawn randomly and observations are independent, and check that the test’s assumptions hold (for example, the t‑test assumes approximately normal data).

例子：某公司欲評估新行銷活動是否提升銷售額。可設定 H₀：新活動與舊活動之間的平均銷售無差異；H₁：新活動平均銷售較高。收集活動前後的銷售資料，計算檢定統計量與 p 值，若 p 值小於 α 則拒絕 H₀，認為新活動有效，否則維持原結論。另一例是臨床試驗測試新藥對血壓的影響，設定 H₀：新藥與安慰劑無差異，H₁：新藥能降低血壓，並使用 t 檢定比較兩組的平均下降值。

Example: A company wants to evaluate whether a new marketing campaign increases sales. We can set H₀: no difference in average sales between the new and previous campaigns, and H₁: average sales are higher under the new campaign. Collect sales data before and after the campaign, compute a test statistic and p‑value, and if the p‑value is less than α, reject H₀ and conclude that the campaign is effective; otherwise retain H₀. Another example is a clinical trial testing whether a new drug lowers blood pressure: set H₀: no difference between the drug and placebo, H₁: the drug lowers blood pressure, and use a t‑test to compare the mean reduction across groups.

程式範例

R 與 Python 範例

以下範例示範如何使用 R 與 Python 進行單樣本 t 檢定並取得 t 統計量與 p 值。

The examples below show how to perform a one‑sample t‑test in R and Python and return the t‑statistic and p‑value.

# R: single-sample t-test
x <- c(5.2, 4.8, 5.1, 4.9, 5.3)
result <- t.test(x, mu = 5)
print(result)
# Python: single-sample t-test
import numpy as np
from scipy import stats
data = np.array([5.2, 4.8, 5.1, 4.9, 5.3])
t_stat, p_value = stats.ttest_1samp(data, 5)
print(t_stat, p_value)
